OpenSummit.AI 2026 - 10 Key Takeaways

Prepared by Mikhail Narbekov, Sydney Town Hall - 23 July 2026

01

Your Data Is the Edge, Not the Model

Models are becoming replaceable commodities. Your proprietary data and context are the real, lasting advantage.

02

Same Task, Up to 24x Price Difference

The same task ran A$0.24 on one model vs A$0.01 on another - a 24x spread. Match the task to the model, not the hype.

03

Today's Top Cloud Models Will Run Locally Soon

Within a year, today's cloud-grade models should run on high-end home computers.

04

Think Task First, Then Pick the Model

Ask "which model for this job", not "which is best". Anthropic for strategy, OpenAI for ops, Gemini for visuals. Don't lock to one vendor.

05

Australia's AI Gap Is Not Access. It Is Application.

Australia is high on basic chatbot use but low on advanced business AI - ranked 9th of 64 (Cybernews AI Adoption Index).

06

AI Complexity Demands Specialists, Not Unicorn Hires

AI is complex - the golf-ball surface analogy. Domain knowledge and specialisation are key.

07

Bots Outnumber Humans Online: Optimise for Agents

Bots may outnumber humans online by 2027 (Cloudflare). As agents become the audience, optimising for them is the new SEO.

08

Agentic Commerce Grows, but Full Autonomy Is Still Scary

AI agents will handle more of the buying journey. Full autonomy - handing over your card unsupervised - still feels a step too far.

09

74% of Companies Are Not Getting Their Expected AI ROI

74% of companies say AI isn't delivering the ROI they expected (McKinsey). ROI is now the boardroom question.

10

OpenAI Earns $1 for Every $4 It Spends

OpenAI reportedly earns about $1 for every $4 it spends - growth funded by heavy compute investment.

Same task. Different model. Different cost.
